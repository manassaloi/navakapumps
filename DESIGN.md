# Navaka website purpose

Navaka is an industrial pump supplier. This website helps buyers identify relevant pumps, assess Navaka's reliability and support, and contact sales about their requirements.

The main journey is: understand the business → explore the product range → review the relevant product → contact sales.

Design decisions must support that journey:
- Keep product discovery and Contact Sales prominent.
- Preserve the original logo and existing business copy.
- Use legible product information, dependable links and accessible mobile layouts.
- Use 3D only as supporting hero imagery. No model-viewer labels, demo controls, exploded views or interaction instructions.
- Keep animation brief and optional through reduced-motion preferences. Never compete with reading or navigation.
- Use a plain background. The featured cards use distinct 3D pump illustrations with catalogue-photo fallbacks; detailed product pages retain real photographs. Procedural illustrations are not model-specific engineering representations.
