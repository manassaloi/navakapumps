## Rotary gear pump range

Rotodel's range includes general-purpose gear pumps, stainless-steel variants, twin gear pumps and high-pressure gear pumps. Model selection depends on the fluid, viscosity, flow rate, pressure and temperature required by your process.

The images above show the **HGN** and **HGBX** series from the Rotodel product catalogue. These are rotary gear pumps.

## Applications

Gear pumps are used to transfer viscous liquids such as oils and other compatible process fluids. Our team can help select the appropriate construction, seals and drive arrangement for your application.
