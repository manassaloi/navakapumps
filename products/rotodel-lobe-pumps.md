## RLP Rotary Lobe Pumps

The RLP is a rotary positive-displacement pump. Rotating lobes move liquid through the pump casing. Selection should take account of your product's viscosity, required flow, discharge pressure and operating temperature.

## Select a pump for your application

Share your fluid and operating conditions with our team. We can help confirm the appropriate pump size, materials, seals and cleaning requirements before recommending a configuration.
