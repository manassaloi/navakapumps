Navaka India has expanded its product range through a business association with Vithram India, adding **hygienic-duty rotary lobe pumps and twin screw pumps** for process applications requiring gentle product handling.

## VVR Rotary Lobe Pumps

{% include product-image.html src="/assets/img/vithram-vvr-lobe-pump.jpg" alt="Cutaway view of the Vithram VVR rotary lobe pump" %}

The VVR series is designed for high-viscosity and shear-sensitive liquids, with efficient, precise flow even at low flow rates.

**Key features:**

- Gentle handling of viscous and shear-sensitive products.
- Universal mounting with vertical and horizontal positions.
- Front-loading inboard mechanical seals.
- Designed for efficient, low-pulsation operation.

## VMV Twin Screw Pumps

{% include product-image.html src="/assets/img/vithram-vmv-twin-screw-pump.jpg" alt="Vithram VMV hygienic twin screw pump" %}

The VMV series is suited to high-discharge-pressure service and gentle product transfer, including operation at high speeds.

**Key features:**

- Gentle product handling even at high speeds.
- Suitable for high-discharge-pressure applications.
- Supports clean-in-place (CIP) transfers at high rotational speeds.
- Designed for low maintenance and long service life.

## Applications

Vithram's hygienic fluid-handling range serves dairy, prepared food, beverage, brewery, personal care, pharmaceutical and biotechnology industries. Our team can help assess which pump suits your product and process requirements.
